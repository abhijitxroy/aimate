# Saved prompts

My Diary → Saved prompts keeps frequently used words, instructions or complete
prompts. Add/edit, search, copy, insert or delete. Insert adds the saved text at
the ask-bar cursor without sending it; review it and press Ask when ready.

Up to 200 records, with 80-character names and 6,000-character text.
Line breaks and indentation are preserved. Records use the account's encrypted
database and portable backup. They are isolated from other users and are not
automatically sent to AI. Use Connections or Private vault for credentials.
